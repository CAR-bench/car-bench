How to read the scores

Pass³ requires all three trials for a task to succeed. Pass@3 requires at least one successful trial.

Pass@1 is the mean single-trial success rate across the benchmark. Successful trials is the raw total across 90 runs.

Success consistency measures how often an initial task success is repeated across the remaining trials. Latency, tokens, and cost measure efficiency; lower is better.

Ranks and color shading compare submissions within the same track. Gray cards indicate that a reliable comparison was unavailable.

Telemetry note: Latency and token figures were self-reported by submissions and could not be independently verified. Cost is estimated from those reported tokens using standardized pricing and a 75% prompt-cache assumption.
