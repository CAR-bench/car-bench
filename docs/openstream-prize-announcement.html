Openstream.ai Prize Sponsorship

Announced during the CAR-bench Challenge session at IJCAI-ECAI 2026.

Sponsor Announcement

Supporting reliable AI agent research

During the in-person CAR-bench Challenge conference session in Bremen, Openstream.ai announced its sponsorship of a $1,500 prize for the winner of each challenge track.

Prize support: Two $1,500 prizes, one for each CAR-bench Challenge track winner.

About Openstream.ai

Openstream.ai is a global enterprise AI company focused on building trustworthy, multimodal, and agentic AI systems that can understand complex real-world environments, reason over multimodal information, and act with greater reliability and explainability.

Its work combines advances in multimodal learning, neuro-symbolic reasoning, knowledge representation, and intelligent agents, supported by an active applied research program.

We thank Openstream.ai for supporting the CAR-bench Challenge and its goal of rigorously evaluating how well AI systems understand and reason about increasingly complex visual, contextual, and interactive environments. Congratulations again to both winning teams.
